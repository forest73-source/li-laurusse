III.

Art & Craft

“ Creating with our hands is an act of prayer and awakening. When the mind falls silent and the hands shape paper, trace a word in ink, bind a journal, pour the wax of a candle for a ritual or lay out on the table the Nat Cards to dialogue with archetypes and ancestors, the soul finally finds room to tell its story. In my vision, Art & Craft is a meditative path to rediscover slowness, beauty and one’s own centre.

In my soul-making, handcraft draws on the artisan philosophy born in the Victorian era, a time I deeply love for its return to the purity of materials, care for detail and respect for natural rhythms. For me Art & Craft is not a mere aesthetic exercise, but a living tool that I weave into my path as a writer and researcher, using it as a favoured channel in my journeys of awakening the mind.

Every sheet of handmade paper, every piece of calligraphy, every bound notebook, every poetic candle and the imaginal reading of the Nat Cards are treasures for those who wish to root themselves in the present, keeping its memories and its poetry.

01

Handmade Paper

Matter that welcomes and keeps thoughts

Born from the slow meeting of water, fibres and ancient gestures, handmade paper is a living surface. Every sheet keeps small imperfections, nuances and deckled edges that speak of patience and presence. It is not just something to write on, but a material that breathes: a delicate womb ready to welcome the purest intentions, memories and thoughts. There are words written in ink and others woven into matter. Here paper takes shape in stories that become little chests of fabric and paper, and the work of the hands becomes meditation.

02

Dedication Poems & Calligraphy

The enchantment of words written with nib or fountain pen to celebrate the Soul

Ink traces light furrows on the page, turning thought into a visible, sacred sign. Dedication poems are born from listening and take shape through the slow rhythm of my fountain pen. Every word traced by hand becomes a unique work, a visual gift created to celebrate a moment, a bond or a rebirth of the Soul.

03

Notebooks and Short Fairy Tales, Hand-bound and Handwritten

Treasure chests for our adult child, keepers of the joy of holding little treasures in our hands

Sewn and bound with artisan care, these notebooks and little handwritten stories are made to give a voice to our adult child. They invite us to rediscover the wonder and pure joy of the days when, as children, we held our most secret treasures in our hands. Little books of affection, made to remind us that the beauty of enchantment has no age.

04

Creative Journal & Scrapbooking

A space for listening, free expression and awakening of the mind

The creative journal is a refuge where words meet matter, colour and the images of the soul. Through scrapbooking and handwork, the mind slows down, judgement falls silent and creativity becomes a practice of meditation and inner awakening. It is an intimate space to give shape to emotions, but also a place of the heart open to the future: a living trace to return to in the dark and sad moments life sometimes brings, to find the way again, shed light and remember who we are.

05

Poetic Candles & Rituals

Flames of light to hold intentions and make room for silence

Handmade with natural waxes, essences and botanical details, poetic candles are created to accompany the moments we devote to ourselves and our small daily rituals. Their flame does not only light a room, but brings light within: a point of fire and warmth to gather around, to meditate, write, entrust an intention to time and rediscover the sacredness of our inner spaces.

06

Imaginal Reading with the Nat Cards

A tool for listening, dialogue with the ancestors and inner peace

Inspired by the nature spirits and by the imaginal method, the Nat Cards are not a simple tool of divination, but a mirror of the Soul. Through their symbols and ancestral archetypes, the reading becomes a sacred encounter in which to explore our roots, untie the knots of family constellations and listen to the guiding voice that lives within us, to find clarity and harmony on our path.

I invite you to slow your pace and leaf through these treasures with your eyes. Whether you would like a unique piece made to measure or wish to write to me to begin a journey of “soul-making” together through art, matter and symbol, this is your refuge.
