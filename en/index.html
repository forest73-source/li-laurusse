Lié Larousse, writer and poet

Bio

“ There are places within us where time slows down to make room for presence and listening.

Lié Larousse

Lié Larousse lives exactly there, among the rhythms of the earth, the magic of small things and the depth of invisible bonds. A novelist and short-story writer, a poet, a practitioner and researcher in psychology and botany, since 2012 Lié has nurtured her digital space “Libroarbitrio – Il tempo di un tè con Lié”, an internationally recognised literary and cultural blog.

Her literary work ranges from poetry, which explores the soul, with the collections .Lié., .la vita comunque. and .Dopo di noi., to fiction, which explores the secret rooms of the mind, with the novels Rid Dementia and NATO SOLO (both awarded for best TV series screenplay at Casa Sanremo Writers), and on to her next novel, out in December: an intense work on our human relationship with pain, mental care and healing through hypnosis. Her path also reaches into the social analysis of artificial intelligence with Ciao, come posso aiutarti? I.A. Innamorati Artificiali.

Her writing is a caress that joins dream and reality, an invitation to tend our roots so that the new can sprout in silence. Exploring the territories of the human soul, Lié brings together her passion for words and for psychology, weaving paths of awakening the mind and of soul-making through Craft Art.
